📷 HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS (super easy!)

1. Copy your couple photos into THIS folder (preeti-birthday/images/)
2. Name them like this:
   photo1.jpg
   photo2.jpg
   photo3.jpg
   ... up to photo20 (jpg / jpeg / png all work!)

   Example: images/photo1.jpg

3. Just refresh the website — they appear automatically in "Our Beautiful Memories"
   + the first photo also appears in the round frame at the top ❤️

ALTERNATIVE (no renaming needed):
- Open the website and tap "➕ Add Our Photos" and select photos from phone/laptop.
  They show instantly!

Tip: use 5-10 best pics, portrait + landscape both look great.
